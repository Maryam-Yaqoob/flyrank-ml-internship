Predicting Search-Visibility Decline Before It Shows Up in the Numbers

A client-grouped classifier for is_declining_next_half — flagging content pages whose impressions are on track to drop more than 20% in the second half of the month, using only signals visible before Mar 16.

Maryam Yaqoob COMSATS University Islamabad Data: fact_content_daily_performance, Mar 2026 Last updated Aug 2026
Abstract

This project asks whether early-month search performance signals can flag content pages likely to lose meaningful impression volume before the month is over. Working from the full FlyRank warehouse release (fact_content_daily_performance, March 2026), the label is_declining_next_half marks pages whose impressions fall more than 20% from the first half of the month (Mar 1–15) to the second (Mar 16–31). A hand-written baseline rule reaches 50.0% Precision@10 against a 32.7% base rate across 151,981 modeling pairs from 55 clients. Logistic Regression and Random Forest models, evaluated on a client-grouped holdout split to prevent cross-client leakage, are compared against that baseline.
